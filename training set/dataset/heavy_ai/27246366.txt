Senior Construction Manager

Summary:
Responsible for the scheduling, coordination, and management of construction projects. Skilled in interpreting and explaining plans and contract terms, preparing and submitting budget estimates, and negotiating revisions to contractual agreements. Proficient in managing a team of 57, overseeing projects valued at $32 million, and achieving a 96.5% success rate on assigned projects. Possess OSHA Certified Forklift and HAZMAT certifications, and experience in residential construction, concrete estimation, permit processing, site safety, and leadership in energy efficient design (LEED).

Key Responsibilities:
- Project Management: Schedules projects in logical steps, prepares contracts, and negotiates revisions, changes, and additions to contractual agreements. Prepares and submits budget estimates, progress reports, and cost tracking reports.
- Site Management: Inspects and reviews projects to monitor compliance with building and safety codes, or other regulations. Ensures compliance with environmental regulations and implements environmental protection programs.
- Team Leadership: Directs and supervises workers, selects and contracts workers for specific tasks, and develops or implements quality control programs. Trains and mentors over 115 construction workers, general laborers, and apprentices.
- Budgeting and Cost Control: Develops or implements construction budgets that compare green and non-green construction alternatives in terms of short-term costs, long-term costs, or environmental impacts. Ensures accurate project progress reporting and monitors safety protocols.
- Site Safety: Oversees the safety of all construction activities, ensuring compliance with safety protocols. Utilizes the Incident and Issues Tracking (IIT) system to document all on-site issues and acts as the liaison with company safety representatives.
- Environmental Compliance: Obtains all necessary permits and licenses, and evaluates construction methods for cost-effectiveness. Implements training programs on environmentally responsible building topics to update employee skills and knowledge.
- Project Closeout: Submits all project closeout documents in accordance with the contract. Manages the rights of way, easement, and dedication processes, and educates general contractor personnel on quality standards throughout the construction process.
